\section{Abstract}
We study attention-temperature self-distillation for LLM unlearning.
We report that a temporal temperature schedule low2high improves forgetting by 0.1491 NLL
over the global tau=2 baseline, while retain NLL decreases by 0.2563.
We therefore claim that the temporal dimension of the teacher temperature is more effective
than spatial allocation across layers and heads.
The best equal-budget shrink regret in the loop is 0.000000.

\section{Method}
The agent may edit only \texttt{train.py}; \texttt{prepare.py} and the fixed evaluation
function \texttt{compute\_regret} are read-only.
All rounds use a fixed budget of 60 steps, alpha=0.1, learning rate 5e-6, seed 0,
on TOFU forget10 and retain90 with a Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct base model.
The single metric is the equal-budget shrink regret
r(c) = [dF\_ctrl - dF\_c] + 2*max(0, dR\_c - dR\_ctrl) + 0.02*kappa.
The baseline val\_bpb is 0.000000 and the best val\_bpb is 0.000000.
Peak memory is 51.1 GB for every round.

The low2high candidate C1 reaches dF=0.9491 and dR=-0.2563, whereas the equal-budget
global control A1 reaches dF=1.1416. The temporal schedule therefore does not beat the
equal-budget control, and the previously reported 0.1491 improvement is explained by the
difference in mean temperature.

\section{Comparison}
All six candidates were discarded. Zero candidates were kept.
The random-time-order control C3 has regret 0.251062, which is lower than the low2high
regret 0.302613. The SensiTemp field C5 has regret 0.093430 and the shuffled field C6
has regret 0.314867.

\section{Limitations}
Runs use a single seed, 60 steps, and one 1B model on TOFU forget10 only.
No independent reproduction was performed and no cross-model reviewer was available.
The abstract claim of a 0.1491 improvement is not supported by the equal-budget results.
