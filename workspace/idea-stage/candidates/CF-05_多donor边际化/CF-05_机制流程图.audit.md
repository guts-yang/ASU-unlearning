## 核心公式（LaTeX，逐条已入图）

1. **① K-donor 配对差**：`$$\Delta A^{(k)}_{\ell,p}=A^{+}_{T,\ell}(p)-A^{-}_{T,(k),\ell}(p),\quad k=1..K,\quad p\in S_f$$$$\text{donor 全来自 TOFU 内部保留集、按作者/别名簇隔离}$$`
2. **② 堆叠与边际均值**：`$$\overline{\Delta A}_{\ell,p}=\frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^{K}\Delta A^{(k)}_{\ell,p},\quad M_{\ell,p}=\big[\Delta A^{(1)}_{\ell,p}\ \cdots\ \Delta A^{(K)}_{\ell,p}\big]$$`
3. **③ SVD 共有子空间**：`$$M_{\ell,p}=U^{(r)}_{\ell,p}\Sigma^{(r)}_{\ell,p}V^{(r)\top}_{\ell,p}+E_{\ell,p},\quad \Delta A_{\text{marg},\ell,p}=U^{(r)}_{\ell,p}U^{(r)\top}_{\ell,p}\,\overline{\Delta A}_{\ell,p}$$$$\Delta A^{(k)}_{\text{priv}}=\Delta A^{(k)}_{\ell,p}-\Delta A_{\text{marg},\ell,p}\ (\text{投影掉})$$`
4. **④ 目标（非 donor）**：`$$\mathcal K=\{h: r_{f,p}\le c,\ s_{d_j,p}\le d_j,\ \lVert\Delta r_{E,p}\rVert\le e\},\quad T_{\ell,p}=\operatorname{stopgrad}\!\left(\Pi_{\mathcal K}[h^{+}_{\ell}(p)-\alpha\widehat{\Delta A_{\text{marg},\ell,p}}]\right)$$`
5. **⑤ 损失 / 反传 / 输出**：`$$\mathcal{L}=\lambda_F\mathcal{L}_F+\beta\mathcal{L}_R,\quad \lambda_{KL}^{(\mathrm{ablation})}=0,\quad \nabla_{\theta}\mathcal{L}\ \text{仅}\ \theta,\quad \Rightarrow\theta^{*}$$`
