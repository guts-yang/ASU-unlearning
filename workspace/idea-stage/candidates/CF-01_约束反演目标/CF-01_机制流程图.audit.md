## 核心公式（LaTeX，逐条已入图）

1. **① 双前向取激活差**：`$$A^{\pm}_{T,\ell}(p)=h_{T,\ell}(X^{\pm}\!\oplus y^{+};p),\quad \Delta A_{\ell,p}=A^{+}_{T,\ell}(p)-A^{-}_{T,\ell}(p),\;p\in S_f$$`
2. **② 约束反演 求解 Δ***：`$$\Delta^{*}_{\ell,p}=\arg\min_{\Delta}\; r_f\!\left(h^{+}_{\ell}(p)+\Delta\right)+\gamma\lVert\Delta\rVert^{2}$$$$\text{s.t.}\; r_f\!\left(h^{+}+\Delta\right)\le c,\; U\!\left(h^{+}+\Delta\right)\ge U\!\left(h^{+}\right)-\delta,\; \langle\Delta,v_{\mathrm{id}}\rangle\le 0,\; \Delta\in\mathrm{Null}(G^{\top}_{\mathrm{retain}}),\; \lVert\Delta\rVert\le\varepsilon$$`
3. **③ 中性约束（非 donor）**：`$$r_{f,p}(h)\le c,\quad s_{d_j,p}(h)\le d_j\;(\forall j),\quad \lVert\Delta r_{E,p}(h)\rVert\le e,\quad p\in S_f$$`
4. **④ patch 因果验证**：`$$\Delta^{*}_{\mathrm{causal}}=\sum_{\ell,p} w_{\ell,p}\Delta^{*}_{\ell,p},\quad w_{\ell,p}=\mathbb{1}\!\left[\mathrm{Eff}^{\mathrm{patch}}_{r_f}(\ell,p)<-\eta_c\ \wedge\ |\mathrm{Eff}^{\mathrm{patch}}_{U}(\ell,p)|<\eta_U\right]$$`
5. **⑤ 目标与前向**：`$$T_{\ell,p}=\operatorname{stopgrad}\!\left(h^{+}_{\ell}(p)+\Delta^{*}_{\mathrm{causal},\ell,p}\right),\quad h_{S,\ell}(X^{+}\!\oplus y^{+};p)$$`
6. **⑥ 损失 / 反传 / 输出**：`$$\mathcal{L}_F(k)=\frac{1}{|\Lambda(k)||S_f|}\sum_{\ell\in\Lambda(k)}\sum_{p\in S_f}\rho_\ell(k)\,\operatorname{dist}\!\left(h_{S,\ell}(p),T_{\ell,p}\right)$$$$\mathcal{L}=\lambda_F\mathcal{L}_F+\beta\mathcal{L}_R,\quad \nabla_{\theta}\mathcal{L}\ \text{仅更新}\ \theta,\quad \theta_0\ \text{frozen},\quad \Rightarrow\ \theta^{*}$$`
