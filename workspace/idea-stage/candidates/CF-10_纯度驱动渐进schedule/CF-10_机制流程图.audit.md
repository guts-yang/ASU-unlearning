## 核心公式（LaTeX，逐条已入图）

1. **① 纯度谱**：`$$\mathrm{pur}_{\ell,p}=\mathrm{AUC}_f(\ell,p)-\mathrm{AUC}_{\text{id}}(\ell,p)\ \text{或}\ w_{\ell,p},\quad \mathrm{pur}_{\ell}=\frac{1}{|S_f|}\sum_{p\in S_f}\mathrm{pur}_{\ell,p},\quad \mathrm{std}_{\text{seed}}(\mathrm{pur}_{\ell})<\sigma_{\text{pur}}$$`
2. **② 数据驱动 Λ(k)**：`$$\Lambda(k)=\left\{\ell:\ \mathrm{rank}(\mathrm{pur}_{\ell})\le n_k\right\},\quad n_k=\left\lceil|\mathcal L|\cdot\frac{k}{K}\right\rceil$$`
3. **③ 层权重 ρ_ℓ(k)**：`$$\rho_\ell(k)=\frac{\exp(\mathrm{pur}_{\ell}/t_\rho)}{\sum_{\ell'\in\Lambda(k)}\exp(\mathrm{pur}_{\ell'}/t_\rho)},\quad S_f\ \text{固定不参与 schedule}$$$$\text{避最浅/末端 20% 层为软先验}$$`
4. **④ 目标（中性约束）**：`$$\mathcal K=\{h: r_{f,p}\le c,\ s_{d_j,p}\le d_j,\ \lVert\Delta r_{E,p}\rVert\le e\},\quad T_{\ell,p}=\operatorname{stopgrad}\!\left(\Pi_{\mathcal K}[h^{+}_{\ell}(p)-\alpha\widehat{\Delta A^{\text{pure}}_{\ell,p}}]\right)$$`
5. **⑤ 损失 / 反传 / 输出**：`$$\mathcal{L}_F(k)=\frac{1}{|\Lambda(k)||S_f|}\sum_{\ell\in\Lambda(k)}\sum_{p\in S_f}\rho_\ell(k)\operatorname{dist}\!\left(h_{S,\ell}(p),T_{\ell,p}\right)$$$$\mathcal{L}=\lambda_F\mathcal{L}_F+\beta\mathcal{L}_R,\quad \nabla_{\theta}\mathcal{L}\Rightarrow\theta^{*}$$`
