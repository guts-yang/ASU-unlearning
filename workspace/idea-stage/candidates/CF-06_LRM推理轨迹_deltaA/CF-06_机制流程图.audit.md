## 核心公式（LaTeX，逐条已入图）

1. **① 反事实轨迹对**：`$$\tau^{+}=(c^{+}_1..c^{+}_T),\ \tau^{-}=(c^{-}_1..c^{-}_T),\quad S_{\text{trace}}=\{t:\ \text{step }t\text{ 含事实绑定}\}$$$$\text{由事实 probe + tokenizer offset 冻结，不随 schedule 变}$$`
2. **② 逐 step 轨迹激活差**：`$$\Delta A_{\text{trace},\ell,t}=h_{T,\ell}(X^{+}\!\oplus\tau^{+};p_t)-h_{T,\ell}(X^{-}\!\oplus\tau^{+};p_t),\quad t\in S_{\text{trace}}$$`
3. **③ 数据驱动 step 权重**：`$$\mathrm{Eff}_{r_f}(t)=\text{patch-forward 在 step }t\text{ 的因果读出变化},\quad \rho(t)=\frac{\exp(\mathrm{Eff}_{r_f}(t)/t_0)}{\sum_{t'\in S_{\text{trace}}}\exp(\mathrm{Eff}_{r_f}(t')/t_0)}$$`
4. **④ 目标（中性约束）**：`$$\mathcal K=\{h: r_{f,p}\le c,\ s_{d_j,p}\le d_j,\ \lVert\Delta r_{E,p}\rVert\le e\},\quad T_{\ell,t}=\operatorname{stopgrad}\!\left(\Pi_{\mathcal K}[h^{+}_{\ell}(p_t)-\alpha\widehat{\Delta A_{\text{trace},\ell,t}}]\right)$$`
5. **⑤ 损失 / 反传 / 输出**：`$$\mathcal{L}_F^{\text{trace}}(k)=\frac{1}{|\Lambda(k)|}\sum_{\ell\in\Lambda(k)}\sum_{t\in S_{\text{trace}}}\rho_\ell(k)\rho(t)\operatorname{dist}\!\left(h_{S,\ell}(p_t),T_{\ell,t}\right)$$$$\mathcal{L}=\lambda_F\mathcal{L}_F^{\text{trace}}+\beta\mathcal{L}_R+\gamma\mathcal{L}_{\text{reason}},\quad \nabla_{\theta}\mathcal{L}\Rightarrow\theta^{*}\ (\text{保推理效用})$$`
